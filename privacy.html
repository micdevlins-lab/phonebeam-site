PhoneBeam Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 November 2026 (revised after review). Applies to the PhoneBeam app for iPhone.

The short version

PhoneBeam has no accounts and no ads, and the app itself sends us no data. We do not receive your screen, your sound or your videos. What you mirror or send goes from your iPhone to your TV over your own Wi-Fi.

What PhoneBeam does with your screen and sound

When you start mirroring, your iPhone records its screen and the sound of the app you are using, turns that into a video stream on the phone, and serves it on your Wi-Fi network to the Chromecast or TV you chose, and refuses requests from other devices on your network. The stream is held briefly in the phone's memory and is not saved. Everything on your screen is shown on the TV, including notifications. It is not sent to us or to any server on the internet. The microphone is never recorded.

Send a video

If you choose a video from Photos or Files, PhoneBeam makes a temporary copy inside the app, serves it to your TV over your Wi-Fi, and deletes the copy when the video ends or is replaced. Any copy left behind is deleted the next time the app opens.

If you paste a direct link to a video, PhoneBeam passes that link to your TV and your TV fetches the video itself. The website hosting that video will see a request from your TV, as it would for any visitor. PhoneBeam does not send us the link.

Your local network

PhoneBeam asks for Local Network access so it can find Chromecast devices on your Wi-Fi and so your TV can fetch the stream from your phone. Device names and addresses are used on your phone only.

Google Cast

PhoneBeam uses Google's Cast software to find and connect to your TV, with its analytics reporting switched off. Your Chromecast or Google TV device is a Google product: it loads Google's standard media player and Google may collect information about the use of that device under its own policy. See Google's Privacy Policy. PhoneBeam is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.

Purchases

The free trial and the unlock are handled by Apple through the App Store. Apple tells the app whether your Apple Account has the trial or the unlock. We do not see your name, your payment details or your Apple Account. Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Diagnostics

PhoneBeam keeps a small technical log on your phone: counts such as frames sent, memory used and connection events, with the times they happened. An export of it also includes the app build, your iOS version, whether a VPN is on and the phone's temperature state. It contains no addresses, no TV names, no titles and nothing from your screen. It leaves your phone only if you choose Export diagnostics in Help and send it somewhere yourself.

What we collect

The app itself sends us nothing, and we run no servers for PhoneBeam. Apple gives every developer overall sales figures and, only if you have chosen to share with app developers in your iPhone settings, crash reports that do not identify you. If you email us for support, we will have your email address and whatever you choose to send, and we use it only to reply. Support email is handled by our email provider and may be stored outside Australia. You can ask to see or correct what we hold, or to have your emails deleted, by writing to us.

Children

PhoneBeam is a general utility and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

There is no account to delete. Removing the app from your iPhone removes everything PhoneBeam stored on it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
