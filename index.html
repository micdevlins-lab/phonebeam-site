PhoneBeam Support

PhoneBeam mirrors your iPhone's screen and sound to a Chromecast over your home Wi-Fi.

My TV does not appear

Check the iPhone and the TV are on the same Wi-Fi. In iPhone Settings, open PhoneBeam and make sure Local Network is on. If a VPN is running, pause it. Then tap the Cast button again.

The TV shows a black picture

Some streaming apps block screen recording, so they show black on every mirroring app. Use that app's own Cast button instead.

The TV is a few seconds behind

That is normal for this kind of mirroring. It is fine for video, photos and browsing, and not suited to fast games.

Sound plays on the phone as well

Turn the iPhone's volume down with the side buttons, then set the TV's volume with its remote.

A video from my phone stopped

Keep PhoneBeam open and the screen on while a video from your phone plays. Videos sent by link keep playing on their own.

My notifications showed on the TV

Mirroring shows everything on your screen. Turn on a Focus such as Do Not Disturb before you start.

I bought the unlock and it is locked again

Open Help in the app, tap the Unlock row, then tap Restore Purchase while signed in to the Apple Account you bought it with.
